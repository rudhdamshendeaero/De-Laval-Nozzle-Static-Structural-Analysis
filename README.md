# De-Laval-Nozzle-Static-Structural-Analysis
ANSYS structural analysis of a launch vehicle nozzle coupled to a solid motor casing under 15 MN thrust. Evaluates von Mises stress, total deformation, and factor of safety, targeting a safety factor of 5 through appropriate support configurations.
**Objective:**

To evaluate the structural integrity of a launch vehicle nozzle coupled to a solid rocket motor casing under a maximum thrust load of 15 MN using ANSYS Mechanical. The analysis aims to determine the maximum von Mises stress, total deformation, and factor of safety under the applied loading and boundary conditions. Fixed supports are to be applied at the nozzle–casing coupling interfaces, and appropriate support configurations are to be investigated to achieve a target factor of safety of 5 while maintaining structural integrity.
